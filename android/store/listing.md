# Google Play listing — Keymivo

**App name (≤30):** Keymivo: AI for Buyer Agents

**Short description (≤80):** The AI copilot for buyer agents — from showing to offer in minutes.

**Full description (≤4000):**
Keymivo is the AI copilot for residential buyer's agents. It remembers everything that happens between the first search and the final offer — what your buyers love, what worries them, and which home is winning.

SHOWING MODE
• Capture notes, quick reactions, photos and voice recordings during every showing — even offline.
• Track each household member's opinion separately.

AI SHOWING ANALYSIS
• Turn notes and recordings into a clear summary, concerns, questions and next steps.
• Buyer Match score for every home, based on your buyer's own priorities.
• Keymivo learns buyer preferences over time and suggests updates you approve.

TOURS & COMPARISON
• Plan tours with drive times and optimized stop order.
• Compare homes side by side: match, monthly cost, must-haves and reactions.

OFFER STRATEGY
• Conservative, Competitive and Strong scenarios with monthly payment, cash to close, appraisal-gap exposure and fit with the buyer's budget.
• Share the strategy to your buyer's portal and see which option they prefer.

BUYER PORTAL
• Invite buyers to rate homes, see their tours and message you.

REPORTS & FOLLOW-UPS
• Branded PDF reports: tour summaries, home-search summaries and offer strategies.
• AI follow-up drafts you review before sending — from your own Gmail.

TEAMS & INTEGRATIONS
• Team plan for up to 5 agents. Follow Up Boss, Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Maps.

Built for the U.S. market, starting in Massachusetts. AI-generated analysis is informational and does not replace professional real estate judgment.
